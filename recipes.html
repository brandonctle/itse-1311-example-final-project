Classic Apple Pie

Ingredients

  • 6 Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 pie crusts

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss the apples with sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Fill the bottom crust, then add the top crust.
  4. Bake for 45 minutes until golden.

Easy Apple Crisp

  1. Slice 4 apples into a baking dish.
  2. Mix oats, brown sugar, flour, and butter.
  3. Crumble the topping over the apples.
  4. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes.
