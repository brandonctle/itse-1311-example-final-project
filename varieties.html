Apple Varieties

There are more than 7,500 apple varieties in the world. Here are six I always keep in my kitchen, and what each one is best for.

My six favorite apple varieties
Variety Flavor Texture Best for My rating (out of 5)
Honeycrisp Sweet, slightly tart Very crisp Eating fresh 5
Granny Smith Tart Firm Pies 4
Gala Mild, sweet Crisp Lunchboxes 4
Fuji Very sweet Juicy Salads 4
McIntosh Tangy Soft Applesauce 3
Pink Lady Sweet-tart Dense Baking 5
Ratings are my personal opinion. Try them all!

How to Pick a Good Apple

  • Choose apples that feel firm and heavy for their size.
  • Skip apples with soft spots or wrinkled skin.
  • Store apples in the fridge to keep them crisp for weeks.
