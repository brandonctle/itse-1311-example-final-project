About Me

My Story

I grew up next to a small family orchard and spent every fall picking, sorting, and (mostly) eating apples. Today I plant trees, volunteer at local farms, and teach people how to grow their own.

My goal is simple: plant one apple tree in every town I visit. So far I'm at 42 trees and counting.

Skills

  • Grafting and pruning fruit trees
  • Baking pies, crisps, and apple butter
  • Pressing cider by hand
  • Leading orchard tours and workshops

Apple Words I Use a Lot

Cultivar
A named variety of apple, like Honeycrisp or Gala.
Grafting
Joining a branch from one tree onto the roots of another.
Heirloom
An older variety that has been grown for generations.

Quick Facts
